## Concept

The promise of differentiable programming is that we can move towards taking the derivatives
of almost arbitrarily complicated computer programs, rather than simply thinking about
the derivatives fo mathematical functions. Differentail programming is the natural
evolution of automatic differentiation (AD, sometimes called algorithmic differentiation).
